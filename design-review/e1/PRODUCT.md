# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and technical collaborators evaluating Daniel Martinez for full-stack development, AI integration and business-systems work. Prospective clients are a secondary audience. They arrive with little time and need to understand Daniel's role, inspect one credible project and open the appropriate CV without waiting for an effect or navigating an agency-style funnel.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate individual, explainable capability through verified work. The composition must establish Daniel as a full-stack developer who connects interfaces, databases, APIs and AI agents, then make projects and the Spanish and English CVs immediately reachable. Success is clarity and trust, not spectacle.

## Brand Personality

Precise, human, accountable. Calm technical confidence with visible authorship and no inflated seniority, metrics or product states.

## Anti-references

- Neon or cyberpunk developer portfolios.
- Skill percentages, commitment counters and technology-logo walls.
- Generic agency landing pages that hide the individual contribution.
- Saturated editorial-magazine styling used as a substitute for project evidence.
- Fake dashboards, invented outcomes and broken placeholder links.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before claims: role, state and source stay distinguishable.
2. Projects before effects: motion supports orientation and never delays reading.
3. The individual stays visible: portrait, authorship and working method are clear.
4. Employment readiness is first-class: ES/EN and both CVs remain one action away.
5. Honest scope builds confidence: demos, pilots and production are never conflated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the composition. Preserve zoom, visible focus, semantic landmarks, keyboard operation, 44px touch targets, readable line lengths and reduced-motion behavior. Spanish and English controls use text labels rather than flags alone.
